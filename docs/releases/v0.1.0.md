# Debussy 0.1.0

The first public build of Debussy, a multi-track audio recorder and mixer for macOS on Apple
silicon. It is early software: expect rough edges, and keep copies of projects you care about.

## What's in it

**Recording, mixing and export**

- Import WAV, FLAC, AAC (`.m4a`) and MP3, mono or stereo, at any sample rate.
- Record mono and stereo tracks from any input device, with input meters and monitoring.
- See tracks as waveforms or spectrograms, zoomed and scrolled.
- Mix with a gain, pan, mute and solo strip per channel, and listen in stereo or mono.
- Export the mix to WAV, FLAC, AAC or MP3 (MP3 needs LAME: `brew install lame`).
- Undo and redo every edit; projects save as `.debussy` bundles in `~/Music/Debussy`.
- Share a project as one `.zip` file, and open such archives.

**Studio tracking**

- The other tracks play while you record, lined up with what you heard.
- A click track with tempo, meter, a choice of sounds and a count-in, and a bars and beats grid.

**Effects and routing**

- A patch board for wiring tracks through mixers, buses, effects and feedback delays to the
  output.
- High-pass and low-pass filters, and a four-band parametric EQ.
- Dynamics: compressor, expander, gate and limiter, with presets and lookahead.
- Amplifier distortion with three shapes, bias and oversampling.
- Delay (tempo sync, ping-pong) and reverb (room and plate), each with a picture of its response.
- Chorus and flanger.

**Editing**

- Select a range across tracks and cut, copy, paste or delete it (⌘X, ⌘C, ⌘V, Delete).

**Expression curves**

- Automation curve lanes with Bézier points, wired on the patch board to drive effect
  parameters over time.

See the [user guide](../user/README.md) for how each part works.

## Known gaps

- Punch-in/out and loop recording (#111); snapping to the grid (#114).
- Moving and trimming clips in the window.
- Dynamics: sidechain input (#133), gain-reduction meter (#134), true-peak ceiling (#135).
- Amplifier tone stack (#137); smooth random LFO shape (#150).
- No MIDI yet: tracks, instruments, recording and hardware output are planned (#169–#177).

## Requirements

- A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).
- macOS 11 Big Sur or later.

## Installing

1. Download `Debussy-0.1.0-macos-arm64.zip` from this release and unzip it.
2. Move `Debussy.app` to your Applications folder.
3. This build is not signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS blocks it the first time.
   To open it:
   - On macOS 14 and earlier, right-click (or Control-click) `Debussy.app`, choose **Open**, then
     **Open** again.
   - On macOS 15 and later, open it once, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**,
     scroll down, click **Open Anyway** beside the Debussy message, and confirm.
   - Or, in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Debussy.app`

macOS asks for microphone access the first time you arm a track to record.
